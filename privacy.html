🚗 Hino's RoadBite — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Hino's RoadBite ("the app") helps you find restaurants, coffee, gas, EV charging, and rest areas ahead of you on a drive. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information. It applies to the web app and the Android app (a wrapper around the same web app).

The short version

  • The app has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • It uses your device's location only while you are using it, to find places near you and ahead of you.
  • Your location and settings are not sent to us. We do not operate a server that stores your data, and we do not sell or share your personal information.

Location

When you tap Start driving, the app asks your device for permission to access your location (GPS). Your current position and direction of travel are used on your device to work out which places are in front of you and to rank them by how little they add to your route. Location access is used only while the app is open and in use; the app does not track your location in the background.

To show place names, ratings, distances, hours, and directions, the app sends search requests (which include the relevant location coordinates) to Google Maps Platform (Maps JavaScript API, Places API, and Directions API). Google's handling of that data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive or store those requests ourselves.

Information stored on your device

Your preferences — such as your destination, saved Home and favorite places, chosen filters, and units — are stored locally in your browser/app storage (localStorage) on your device. They stay on your device, are not transmitted to us, and are removed if you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

Information we collect

None. The app has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no server that receives your data. We do not build a profile of you.

Permissions (Android)

  • Location (fine & coarse) — to find places near and ahead of you, as described above.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes
